# FavLock – Private Bookmark Manager

## Short description

Save and update bookmarks, organize with collections, tags and Lists, capture articles, and sync encrypted content across devices.

## Detailed description

FavLock helps you capture bookmarks and articles without breaking your browsing flow, then keeps them organized in one private library. Connect with Google or email, then unlock your encrypted library with a saved passkey or recovery key.

Open the extension to see whether the current page is already in FavLock. Save a new bookmark or update its title, Collection, tags, and Lists without creating another copy. You can also import your existing Chrome bookmarks, save the current window as a tab session, or turn an article into a clean reading view.

Features:

- See when the current page is already saved
- Save new bookmarks or update their organization in place
- Choose or create encrypted Collections, tags, and Lists
- Save all open tabs as one session without duplicating existing bookmarks
- Open Quick Save from Chrome’s right-click menu
- Read articles in a focused, distraction-free view
- Adjust reader text size and choose a light, paper, or dark theme
- Save encrypted article snapshots to Readspace
- Import Chrome bookmarks with duplicate detection
- Optionally open FavLock in every new tab
- Build ordered Lists of videos and articles and track completion in FavLock

FavLock encrypts protected bookmark fields, including titles and URLs, along with Collection, tag, and List names before storage. Saved article snapshot content is also encrypted before it is stored or synced.

The extension uses current-page information when you open Quick Save, open-tab information when you save a tab session, article content when you choose Reader, and your Chrome bookmark tree when you start an import. Protected data you choose to save is encrypted before it is sent to your FavLock account; Reader extraction itself runs in your browser.

A FavLock account and unlocked library are required for saving, updating, and syncing content. Plan limits may apply.
